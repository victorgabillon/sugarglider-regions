Sugarglider privacy policy

Public developer name: Sugarglider.

Publisher and data controller: Victor Gabillon.

Privacy contact: VictorGabillon@gmail.com.

Effective date: 16 September 2026. This policy describes Sugarglider for Android, version 1.0.2 (version code 4).

Local route planning

Sugarglider helps you plan outdoor routes. After a supported region is downloaded, the Android planner calculates routes on your device using installed map, routing, place and nature data. Normal route planning does not send your route to a Sugarglider routing or sharing server. GPX inspection also runs locally.

Private foreground location

With your permission, the planner uses device location for private foreground map positioning: showing your current position and centering or following the map. If permission was already granted, private location can resume when you reopen the planner. You can revoke location permission in Android settings and continue planning manually.

This release does not send live location to a Sugarglider sharing service and does not run a location-sharing foreground service. It does not request background location permission. Social sharing, outings and live-location sharing are unavailable in this release. There is no configurable sharing server, supported sharing backend or Sugarglider social account in version 1.0.2.

Regional downloads and hosting

Regional files and this privacy page are publicly hosted on GitHub Pages. Distribution archives are also stored on GitHub Releases. These hosting services are provided by GitHub. The app downloads regional files from the existing public GitHub Pages hosting over HTTPS.

When you download regional files, check their availability or open this privacy page, GitHub receives connection and request metadata such as your IP address, the requested region or file, request time and device or browser information sent with the request. GitHub processes this information under its privacy statement. Regional download requests do not upload your planned routes or private device-location coordinates. Local planning does not require a routing server, and no Sugarglider social or sharing backend is provided for this release.

Local storage, exports and deletion

Installed regional data and stored planner preferences remain on your device. Use the region removal controls to remove installed regions. Clearing the app's storage/data in Android settings or uninstalling the app removes its local application data.

When you export GPX, you choose the destination using Android's document picker. The storage provider or app you select controls the exported copy and may sync it according to its settings. Removing Sugarglider does not delete exported copies; manage those copies through the destination provider or app.

Advertising, analytics and contact

Version 1.0.2 contains no advertising or analytics integration. This does not prevent the public hosting services from processing connection metadata as described above.

Contact the publisher at the email address above for privacy questions. If you contact us, you choose what information to include in your message. Do not send sensitive location information unless necessary and requested through an appropriate channel.
